FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How can I pay?

Cash, bank transfer, or card (credit card or Visa). Card payments have a 5% surcharge.

Can I shower after the massage?

Yes, you can take a shower after your massage.

Can I leave my luggage?

Yes. Leave your luggage with us, go sightseeing and pick it up later.

Do you offer pick-up?

Yes, free for 90-minute massages: within Hoi An for 2+ guests, one way between Da Nang and Hoi An for 2+ guests, and both ways for 5+ guests.

What are your opening hours?

We're open 24 hours, every day.

Do you have services for kids?

Yes, kids' massage and kids' nail polish, so the whole family can relax together.
